Vector Siege Privacy Policy

Overview

This policy applies to the Vector Siege app, a free, ad-supported mobile game by Pineforge Studios. The app does not require an account or any personal information provided directly to us.

Effective 2026-06-11.

Advertising

To keep the app free, Vector Siege shows ads via Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect:

  • A device advertising identifier (Advertising ID)
  • IP address (and coarse location derived from it)
  • Device information and app interaction / diagnostic data

This data is used to deliver, personalize, frequency-cap, and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Pineforge Studios does not itself collect, store, or sell this data.

Third-Party Services

Vector Siege uses Google AdMob. You can review Google's data use here:

  • https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Consent (EEA, UK & Switzerland)

Where required, the app shows a consent prompt (Google User Messaging Platform) before serving personalized ads. You can change your choice at any time via the privacy options in the app menu.

Advertising ID

You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads).

Children

Vector Siege is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Data Retention

Pineforge Studios retains no personal data through the app. Google retains ad data in accordance with its own policies.

Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted here with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding privacy, please contact: hello@pineforgestudios.com
